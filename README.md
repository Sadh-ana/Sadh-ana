## ✦ A little about me

I'm Sadhana, a high-school student who likes bringing ideas to life through code. I'm interested in the overlap between technology, creativity, psychology, and play — especially tools that help people think, learn, plan, or explore something new.

I learn best by making things. That means taking an idea from a rough concept to a working prototype, figuring out what feels confusing, and iterating instead of waiting until I know everything. I'm still growing as a developer; this profile is a record of what I'm learning and building along the way.

## ⟡ What I like building

- **Useful web experiences:** interfaces that feel clear, thoughtful, and easy to move through.
- **Idea and productivity tools:** ways to collect thoughts, organize information, and turn plans into action.
- **Creative experiments:** playful visuals, small interactions, and ideas that make software feel more human.
- **Games and interactive systems:** rules, quests, progression, and little worlds people can explore.

## ☾ What I want to explore next

- **AI that genuinely helps:** more thoughtful AI features, LLM-powered tools, and interfaces that make AI useful rather than decorative.
- **Game development:** richer mechanics, progression, quests, and small interactive worlds.
- **Creative coding and motion:** subtle animations, generative visuals, and delightful micro-interactions.
- **Data and visual thinking:** turning complex information into interfaces that are easier to understand.
- **Human-centered design:** learning more about psychology, accessibility, and why some digital experiences feel intuitive while others don't.
